# Brand assets

What carries the brand rather than the interface. Each project keeps the sections it needs and removes the rest.

### Rules

- Payment, social and app store marks come from Core [Library], never redrawn or pasted here. A missing mark is added to Core.
- Logotype and illustrations ship with a `viewBox` only, the space they sit in sets the size.
- Favicon files export from their frames as they are.
